    Suggested classroom routine

    Use each visualization as an analysis checkpoint

    1. Predict. Ask what happens next before advancing.
    2. Step. Move one meaningful operation at a time.
    3. Count. Keep the slide’s basic operation visible.
    4. Generalize. Turn the observed pattern into a sum or recurrence.
    5. Compare. Change the input to discuss best, average, or worst cases.
